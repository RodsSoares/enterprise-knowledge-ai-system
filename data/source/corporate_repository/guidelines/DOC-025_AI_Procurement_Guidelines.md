## 1. Purpose

These guidelines help Procurement teams evaluate commercial and operational considerations when acquiring AI-enabled products or services. They are guidance and do not replace corporate policy, security standards, or approval authority.

## 2. Procurement considerations

Procurement should identify the provider, commercial model, expected users, intended business use, data categories, integration requirements, support model, portability, termination provisions, and material subcontractors.

## 3. AI supplier due diligence

Commercial review should consider service commitments, data-processing terms, retention options, intellectual-property provisions, model or service changes, audit rights where applicable, and exit requirements.

## 4. Approval boundary

These guidelines do **not** authorize a new AI tool. Formal approval is governed by the AI Governance Policy and Approval Authority Matrix. Information Security determines applicable security requirements.

## 5. Supplier data

The fact that an AI product is procured does not mean confidential supplier or customer data may automatically be entered into it. Data classification and approved-use requirements remain applicable.

## 6. Evaluation role

This document intentionally contains substantial AI, supplier, data, approval, and procurement terminology. It is a useful semantic neighbor, but it is not the primary authority for security controls or AI approval decisions.
